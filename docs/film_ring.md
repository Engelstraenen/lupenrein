# Einstiegsfilm „Der schwebende Ring“ (Storyboard H1, Eugen 25.11.2026)
Ein durchgehender Film aus 3 Clips, nahtlos angesetzt: Clip B beginnt auf dem letzten Bild von A, Clip C auf dem letzten Bild von B und endet auf dem Galerie-Startbild LRB01 (damit geht der Film ohne Schnitt in die Seite über).
Dreamina Seedance, 16:9 · 720P, Schalter Unlimited an (0 Credits), Modus „First and last frames“ (nur First Frame, bei C zusätzlich Last Frame). Danach lokal schärfen (`tools/schaerfen.py`), Freigabe von Eugen, erst dann ggf. Upscale/2K nach OK.

## Clip A · Ring im Strahl (10 s) · First Frame = gewähltes Startbild (LRF1/2/3)
ACTION STARTS IMMEDIATELY IN THE VERY FIRST FRAME, no still moment. One continuous shot, no cuts. A white gold diamond solitaire ring floats freely in mid-air inside a single hard white beam of light in an almost black room with deep cobalt blue shadows. From the first frame the ring rotates slowly around its vertical axis (exactly one half turn over 10 seconds) while the camera slowly dollies forward toward it, until the ring fills about 60 percent of the frame height at the end. Fine dust particles drift through the beam. Each time the diamond faces the light it flashes crisp spectral rainbow sparks; a bright reflection line glides along the polished band. Camera locked on the ring, focus on the ring at all times, the ring stays centered. Exactly one ring with one diamond and six prongs during the whole shot. Style: luxury jewelry commercial, ARRI Alexa, anamorphic, real optics, razor sharp. Avoid: hands, strings, stands, cushions, extra rings, morphing metal, melting prongs, text, logos, lens flare overload.

## Clip B · Makro (8 s) · First Frame = letztes Bild von Clip A
ACTION STARTS IMMEDIATELY IN THE VERY FIRST FRAME. One continuous shot, no cuts. The camera keeps moving forward from the first frame into an extreme macro of the same ring: it glides past the six platinum prongs, slowly orbits about 40 degrees around the brilliant diamond while the facets light up one after another in pure spectral fire, then follows the mirror-polished band where a single bright reflection line travels along the metal. The ring keeps rotating very slowly. Same ring, same diamond, same prongs as in the first frame, never changing shape. Black-cobalt background, single hard white light from above, dust particles out of focus. Style: high-end jewelry commercial macro cinematography, razor sharp. Avoid: morphing, extra stones, melting metal, hands, text, logos.

## Clip C · Öffnung zur Galerie (8 s) · First = letztes Bild B · Last = LRB01 (Galerie-Startbild)
ACTION STARTS IMMEDIATELY IN THE VERY FIRST FRAME. One continuous shot, no cuts. The camera pulls back from the macro of the ring, the floating ring becomes smaller in the beam, the beam widens and grows brighter until the darkness opens like a curtain of light and reveals a bright white luxury jewelry gallery corridor with glass vitrines and cobalt velvet niches, ending exactly on the last frame. Smooth, elegant, continuous camera move. Avoid: cuts, flashes to white, morphing, people, text, logos.
