ACTION STARTS IMMEDIATELY IN THE VERY FIRST FRAME, no still moment. One single continuous macro shot, no cuts. A white gold engagement ring with a large emerald-cut diamond held by four double claws, in an almost completely black studio. From the very first frame the ring rotates slowly and smoothly around its vertical axis, turning about 90 degrees over the 8 seconds, so the step facets, the claws and the polished band are revealed one after another. At the same time a narrow, hard slit of white light slowly sweeps across the ring from left to right: the step facets of the emerald cut light up one after another like a hall of mirrors and flash with natural diamond fire, a thin clean highlight line glides along the white gold band. The camera stays in extreme macro and glides very slowly closer. Everything outside the light stays deep black with a faint cobalt undertone. Camera locked on the diamond, focus on the diamond at all times, the ring stays centered. Exactly one ring with one emerald-cut diamond and four double claws during the whole shot, the shape never changes. Style: Cartier and Graff luxury commercial, ARRI Alexa, 100mm macro lens, razor sharp, real optics, high-end, elegant, slow and calm. Avoid: hands, stands, strings, extra stones, morphing metal, melting claws, glow, starburst sparkle stars, lens flares, text, logos.
