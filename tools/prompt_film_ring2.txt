ACTION STARTS IMMEDIATELY IN THE VERY FIRST FRAME, no still moment. One single continuous macro shot, no cuts. The camera keeps pushing forward steadily into the emerald-cut diamond of the white gold ring: from the first frame the table facet grows, after 3 seconds it fills the whole frame, then the camera passes through the table into the inside of the stone: long parallel step facets like a hall of mirrors stretch into depth and slide past the camera, one narrow beam of pure white light travels through them and splits into fine spectral colours only along the facet edges, the reflections multiply symmetrically. The last 2 seconds end on a bright, symmetric field of crystalline facets and white light. Deep black around the stone at the start, faint cobalt undertone.
